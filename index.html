Luqma Privacy Policy

Effective 21 November 2026. Developer: Muhammad Zubair (ZubairLabs).

Luqma stores food diary entries, nutrition values, portions, water logs, optional calorie target, favourites and interface preferences in private storage on this device. This can include health-related information entered by you. The app does not send these records to the developer and has no Internet permission, account system, advertising or analytics SDK.

The app does not access your camera, microphone, location, contacts or bank information. Android's file picker is used only when you request a JSON backup, CSV export or backup restoration. You choose the location and files. If you choose a cloud storage provider, that provider's own terms and privacy practices apply. Exported files are readable and not encrypted by Luqma; keep them private.

You may delete individual diary entries and custom foods, or clear all local data from Settings. Uninstalling or clearing Android app storage removes records. Exported copies remain until you delete them separately. No remote account exists to delete and no cloud synchronization is provided.

The app is for general adult food awareness. It does not provide medical advice, diagnose conditions, prescribe calorie targets, calculate medication doses or perform camera food analysis. Nutrition values are estimates, with source details included in the app.

Contact: questions about this policy or the app can be sent to xubbi.official@gmail.com.
